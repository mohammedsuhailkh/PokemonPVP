## Grass prompt

Reference: approved concept.

> Create the clean background asset for this approved game design. Preserve exactly this high-angle near-overhead stadium viewpoint, framing, shallow trapezoidal field, wide landscape aspect, painted anime grass textures, narrow strip of stands at the top, pale stadium wall and blue banners. Remove ALL interface: health panels, names, timer, buttons, text, icons. Remove both Pokémon and their shadows. Remove ALL white painted field lines including football boxes and center emblem; leave a completely unmarked open grassy field because the game engine will draw its own Pokémon arena lines. The central 85 percent must be empty playable grass with no rocks or obstacles. Retain spectator stands only in the narrow top 12 percent and subtle walls at the far left/right outside the playable court. Bright even sunny lighting. This is an empty 2D painted stadium backdrop, not a screenshot, not a football field, no sky, no horizon, no characters. Keep the source composition and camera.

Produce an empty scene asset matching the grass stadium's exact high camera and broad playing-space proportions.

Replace the grass playing surface with a broad continuous flat pale blue ice court, softly textured frost and restrained faint blue cracks. The engine adds actual gameplay ice and water hazards separately. Match the same tiered crowd wall at the top, now pale icy stone and cyan/blue banners with cool white lights. Clean bright cool anime painting, soft reflected daylight, legible under small colorful sprites. No Pokémon, no characters, no UI, no text, no buttons, no field markings, no white boundary lines, no center emblem, no sky or horizon. Same layout as the reference so a 2D game can swap backgrounds while retaining identical world projection.
