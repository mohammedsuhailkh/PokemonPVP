The `effects/` compiled `.efkwgd` effects and `runtime/` WebGPU/WASM files
are copied from the above revision. The upstream repository does not include
a root license file at this revision; this notice does not assign a new license
to those files. Upstream author and third-party rights remain with their owners.

`atlases/*.webp` are derived renderings of the actual effects using the included
upstream runtime, generated by `scripts/bake-showmotion.mjs`. The baking script
uses the upstream camera, samples motion, removes the black render backing,
crops transparent borders, and packs frames for Canvas playback. The original
compiled effects are unmodified. Missing moves reuse these rendered motions
with type-based hue, saturation, size, rotation, and timing changes.

No browser-extension permissions or injected Pokémon Showdown scripts are used
by this game's runtime. Playing the animations does not require WebGPU, WASM,
a browser extension, or a connection to GitHub.
