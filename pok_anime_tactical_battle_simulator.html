2.5D REAL-TIME ANIME STADIUM CONTROLS

1. Full Two-Handed Keyboard Controls

  • Move (360°): W A S D or Arrow Keys
  • Unleash Moves 1–4: 1 2 3 4 or Arcade Keys J K L U
  • Dodge Dash (i-Frames): SPACE or SHIFT
  • Counter-Shield / Guard: E (Counter) • Hold Q (Guard)
  • Switch Pokémon / Overdrive: Z X C or TAB • R (Overdrive) • F (Aim Mode)

2. Counter-Shield [E] & Guard [Q]

Press E to spin and unleash Ash's Sinnoh Counter-Shield—a 360° vortex that destroys incoming enemy projectiles, repels melee blitzes, and deals chip damage! Hold Q (or Right-Click) to raise an armored hexagonal barrier that cuts incoming damage by 65%.

3. Spatial Move Archetypes [1-4]

  • BEAM: Projects a warning laser before firing a massive arena-spanning beam you can physically sidestep!
  • ORB / BARRAGE: Launches high-speed homing or spread projectiles.
  • BLITZ: Rockets across the turf wrapped in elemental aura for a melee slam!
  • AOE WAVE: Detonates an expanding 360° stadium shockwave.

4. Interactive Stadium Hazards

  • Water Puddles + Electric: Hitting a soaked puddle with an Electric move electrifies the water into a 1.35x high-voltage zone!
  • Scorched Magma: Fire attacks leave burning turf patches.
  • 2.5D Rock Pillars: Heavy physical/ground hits raise rock pillars you can hide behind to block enemy beams!
